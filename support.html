Common questions

Headlines aren't flipping

Some launchers don't let widgets update themselves on a timer, so the headline stays put. Use the arrows on the widget to move between headlines instead.

How do I restore my purchase?

You don't need to do anything. If you reinstall FeedTicker or move to a new phone, the unlock restores automatically as long as you use the same Google account.

A site I added says no feed was found

Not every website publishes an RSS or Atom feed. Try pasting the feed's own address if you know it (often ending in /feed, /rss or .xml). For Reddit, type the subreddit like r/space.

Headlines look out of date

FeedTicker refreshes in the background about every 30 minutes and shows cached headlines in the meantime, so it works offline. Tap the refresh button on the widget to fetch new headlines now.

How do I add more than two feeds?

The free version supports up to 2 feeds. The one-time unlock removes the limit and adds the Advanced settings. You can have as many widgets as you like either way.

Can different widgets show different feeds?

Yes. Each widget has its own feeds and style.

How do I delete my data?

Everything FeedTicker stores is on your phone. Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android's settings, and it's gone. See the privacy policy.
