FeedTicker Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 September 2026
Last updated: 23 September 2026

This policy covers FeedTicker, an Android home-screen widget made by Calm Kraken Studios (“we”, “us”). It explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

Summary

  • FeedTicker does not collect, store or share any personal data.
  • There is no account or sign-in, and no analytics, ads, crash reporting or tracking.
  • Your feeds and settings stay on your device.
  • The app only contacts the websites whose feeds you add.

Information we collect

None. FeedTicker has no account or sign-in. It contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs. Nothing about you or how you use the app is sent to Calm Kraken Studios.

Network requests the app makes

FeedTicker connects to the internet only to:

  • Fetch the feeds you add. The app requests each feed URL you add, both when you add it and during background refreshes (about every 30 minutes).
  • Find a site's feed. When you enter a website address rather than a feed URL, the app requests that site to find its RSS or Atom feed.

If you enter a subreddit such as r/space, the app fetches that subreddit's public feed from Reddit.

These requests go straight from your device to the website. Like any web request, it reveals your IP address and the app's user agent to that website. How each website handles that information is covered by its own privacy policy. We don't receive or see these requests.

Information stored on your device

The following are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • your list of feeds
  • your widget and app settings
  • cached headlines and article previews
  • any custom font file you choose to load

None of this is sent to us. Uninstalling FeedTicker deletes all of it. You can also delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android's settings.

Purchases

The optional unlock is sold through Google Play and processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see your payment details. The app only learns whether the unlock is owned by the Google account on the device. Google's handling of your purchase is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Opening articles

When you tap a headline, FeedTicker opens the article in your own web browser. From then on, the article's website and your browser handle your data under their own privacy policies.

Children

FeedTicker is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

If you email us

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message. We use it only to reply, and we don't share it with anyone.

This website

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or third-party scripts. If you switch between light and dark themes or pick a colour, that choice is saved in your browser’s local storage and never sent anywhere. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and request times, to operate and secure the service.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version on this page and update the “Last updated” date. If a change affects what data the app handles, we'll describe it here before it takes effect.
